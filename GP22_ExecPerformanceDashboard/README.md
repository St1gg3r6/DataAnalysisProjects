# Executive Revenue & Profit Performance Dashboard

## AdventureWorks Retail Case Study

### Business Problem

A retail organisation required improved visibility into revenue, profitability, and return performance to support commercial and operational decision-making. Leadership needed a consolidated executive view of sales performance, product trends, customer behaviour, and return impact.

This dashboard was developed to provide structured, KPI-driven performance monitoring across products, regions, and customer segments.

⸻

### Objectives
* Monitor total revenue, profit, and order volume
* Track return rate impact on profitability
* Analyse monthly sales and order trends
* Identify top-performing and underperforming product categories
* Enable customer-level and product-level performance analysis

⸻

### Executive Dashboard Overview

The Executive Dashboard provides a high-level commercial performance summary including:
* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Order Count
* Return Rate
* Monthly revenue, orders, and returns
* Revenue trend analysis
* Orders by product category
* Top 10 products by revenue

This page is designed for senior stakeholders requiring quick, actionable insight into business performance.

⸻

### Product & Customer Analysis

Additional report pages provide deeper analysis:

#### Product Detail
* Revenue and profitability by product
* Category-level performance
* Identification of top revenue drivers

#### Customer Detail
* Customer purchasing behaviour
* Revenue concentration analysis
* Order frequency and contribution

#### Geographic Performance (Map View)
* Regional distribution of revenue
* Geographic performance trends

⸻

### Key Analytical Focus Areas

* Revenue vs Profit alignment
* Return rate impact on margin
* Category contribution to total revenue
* Concentration of revenue among top products

⸻
