# New Coder Market Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes data from freeCodeCamp's 2017 New Coder Survey to identify the best markets for an e-learning company to advertise its programming courses. The analysis focuses on understanding new coders' interests, locations, and willingness to invest in their education, with the goal of recommending the top two regions for advertising.

## Data Source

- [2017 New Coder Survey](https://github.com/freeCodeCamp/2017-new-coder-survey)
- Survey completed by thousands of new coders worldwide

## Contents

- **Introduction:** Project background and objectives
- **Data Exploration:** Initial inspection and cleaning of the survey dataset
- **Interest Analysis:** Investigation of respondents' job role interests
- **Location Analysis:** Identification of countries with the highest concentration of new coders
- **Spending Analysis:** Calculation of average monthly spend on learning by country
- **Outlier Handling:** Iterative removal of extreme values to refine insights
- **Market Recommendations:** Data-driven suggestions for advertising investment

## Key Findings

- The United States has the largest population of new coders and the highest average monthly spend on learning.
- India, Canada, and the United Kingdom are also significant markets, but further investigation is needed to determine the optimal secondary region for advertising.
- Most respondents are interested in web and mobile development, aligning well with the company's course offerings.
